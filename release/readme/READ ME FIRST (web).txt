LAKAY ON YOUR OWN WEBSITE
Build @@BUILD@@, @@DATE@@.

PUT IT ONLINE
1. Unzip this file. You get the folder "lakay" and this note.
2. Upload the whole "lakay" folder to your website, beside your other pages
   (with your host's File Manager, or by FTP).
3. Open https://YOUR-DOMAIN/lakay/ in a browser.

To have Lakay at the main address (https://YOUR-DOMAIN/) instead, upload what is
inside the "lakay" folder rather than the folder itself. Do this only if nothing
else lives there: it would replace an index.html that is already there.

THE ADDRESS MUST BEGIN WITH https://
Lakay seals the store's books, and browsers allow that only on a secure address.
Most hosts give https for free; turn it on in your host's settings if it is not
on yet. On an address that begins with http:// Lakay will say so and not open.

ON A PHONE, A TABLET OR A COMPUTER
- iPhone and iPad: open the address in Safari, press Share, then
  "Add to Home Screen". Lakay's logo goes on the home screen.
- Mac: open it in Safari, then File, "Add to Dock". The logo goes in the Dock.
- Windows, Mac or Android with Chrome or Edge: choose "Install Lakay" in the
  address bar or in the browser's menu.
The logo also shows in the browser's tab and address bar.
Once Lakay has been opened, it opens again with no connection.

THE BOOKS STAY ON EACH DEVICE
Nothing the store types is sent to your website or anywhere else. Each browser,
and each Lakay added to a home screen or the Dock, keeps its own books, sealed
with the store's password. Use one place for the store's books on each device,
and keep a backup (Settings, Backups).

A NEW BUILD
Upload the new "lakay" folder over the old one. Each device takes the new build
the next time Lakay opens with a connection. The books are not touched.
