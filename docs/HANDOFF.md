## How the app is made

- One HTML page built by `build.py` from `src/js/*.js` and `src/css/*.css`, in name order (`z-door.css` last).
- A strict Content Security Policy is used: no eval, no innerHTML. `E.util.h()` builds every element.
- The store's books are sealed with AES-GCM in IndexedDB, with a hash-chained ledger. Every change is one ledger line stamped with `by` (the user id) and the device.
- The old names stay where data lives, so nothing is lost: IndexedDB `emporium`, localStorage `emporium.`, the Fire and iPhone bridge `EmporiumHost`, and the data folders named Emporium.
- **Modules:**
  - `09-crest.js` + `crest.css`: the living mark, sixteen planes at real depth (the pictures are in `src/brand/lakay-mark`, eleven roles by day and by night).
  - `11-door.js`: Ingenium's introduction door with its lock pill, the sign-in in the card's place, the set-up with the Master Key.
  - `17-panel.js`: the Offline Project's edit menu (`#cd-em-menu`) and the device clipboard bridges.
  - `18-peek.js`: the show-password key on every password field.
  - `19-sound.js`: live sounds.
  - `05-find.js`: the finder, one index of the items in memory (never written into the books): word search, codes (a code finds its item as EAN-13, UPC-A, EAN-8 or GTIN-14), each department's figures. Every list that can grow large asks it (Inventory, the Register, Price check).
  - `13-inventory.js` + `room-inventory.css`: Inventory's tabs and department pages, the list a page of fifty at a time, the item form (a barcode field a scan fills, with the camera beside it), and the money transfers and top-ups (`B.recordService`, `B.voidService` in `04-books.js`: record kind `svc`, ledger lines `service.recorded` and `service.voided`, replayed by Verify; `B.report().services` keeps them out of the sales).
  - `23-scan.js`: scanning. A scanner that types is told from a person by its speed (a code's keys within about 45 ms of each other, then Enter or Tab), only when no field has the focus; each room says what a code does there (`ui.onScan`). The camera: the device's `BarcodeDetector` where it has one, otherwise Lakay's own reader (EAN-13, UPC-A, EAN-8; check digit; the same code twice).
  - `20-rail.js` + `rail.css`: the side rail, three keys since Build 14 (Schedae, a new sale, Price check); the reminders (`E.rail.reminders`, drawn inside the Alerts panel, `ui.alertsPanel` in `10-ui.js`) and the floating calculator (`E.rail.calc`, opened by the top bar's `#calcBtn`).
  - `src/web/sw.js` and the end of `build.py`: the web app in `web/` (the page with the web's own policy, the manifest, the icons from `release/icons.py`, the service worker). `99-boot.js` registers the worker only in the web build (`meta[name="lakay-web"]`) and only over https or on this computer.
  - `22-schedae.js` + `schedae.css`: Schedae, Carpe Diem's sticky notes. Storage is in `04-books.js` (`B.notes`, `B.saveNotes`: one sealed record per person, written quietly, never a ledger line); `D.writeTo` in `03-db.js` writes into the books a note was made in. The paper's values were measured from Carpe Diem (day and night computed styles).
  - `21-team.js` + `team.css`: the Team room (who is on the clock, time cards, the people with their IDs and access). The people, their envelopes, the sign-in (`B.signIn`), access (`B.can`) and the time clock (`B.clockIn`, `B.clockOut`, `B.breakStart`, `B.breakEnd`) are in `04-books.js`; the status key and sign-out in `10-ui.js`; the first own password at the door in `11-door.js`. See `docs/02-DESIGN-AND-SECURITY.md`, "The people's own keys".
- **The mark is the Spiral.** It was chosen and approved. Its drawing sources are in `docs/brand/source` (`gen3.py`, `spiral9` draws the planes). Le Comptoir's compass rose and the other marks are kept in `docs/brand/backup`.

## Rules the owner has set

- **Delivery:** the ready-made apps download from GitHub the way the other apps' do. No browser page in place of an app; no other way around.
- **Never locked out:** the owner must always be able to get in with the password alone, reach the practice store from the door, and reset without a password. Keep every new sign-in rule to this (Build 7).
- **Look:** Always hide scrollbars. Keep parity with Carpe Diem and Ingenium where the owner asks for it. Show designs before committing to them.
- **The Home's surfaces:** white slabs, with the cards inside them and the keys that act in the enamel, or a gentle lavender beside it, as Ingenium and Carpe Diem do (the owner, 14 November 2026). Softer and subtle, still sleek and elegant.
- **Colours:** never green for Lakay's design (the owner rejected a green Home outright, 13 November 2026, and it was never released). Since Build 15 even the "all is well" colour (`--ok`) is the logo's periwinkle. Lakay's colours come from its seashell logo: periwinkle enamel, the shell's indigo, pearl, blush, and the champagne gold of the rim.
- **The logo on the Home:** alone, with its living, popped-out 3D mark; never a ring, rim or disc drawn around it (the owner, 14 November 2026).
- **The colour, woven in:** on the white slabs, the periwinkle goes in rims, slim bands, edges and jewels, never as thick solid purple cards or keys (the owner, 14 November 2026). No gold band at the foot of the Home (the owner found it gaudy and oddly placed).
- **The side rail stays uncrowded:** only the keys the counter reaches for all day (the sticky notes, a new sale, Price check). Anything else goes in the top bar: Alerts (what needs seeing to and the reminders), the calculator, Display (the owner, 14 November 2026).
- **The web app:** the same page as the apps, from `build.py`, delivered as `builds/Lakay-Web.zip` for the owner's own website; its policy may add only `'self'` for its own manifest (`manifest.json`), icons and worker; the worker keeps the site's own files and never the books. It needs https. Its icons follow the rule below.
- **The logo alone:** every icon shows the logo with no white square around it (the owner, 13 November 2026). Where a system must draw a square (the iPhone and iPad home screen), fill it edge to edge with the logo's own periwinkle. Make icons with `release/icons.py` from the one approved drawing.
- **Names and imagery:** never fruit, food or other stereotyped names or images for the app's colours and designs (the owner, 13 November 2026). Keep them professional and elevated: the notes' papers are named for gems.
- **Lakay's own design:** keep it, and elevate it; do not copy the sister apps' surfaces (the owner, 13 November 2026). Not glossy, not puffy: crisp hairline rims, firm stepped edges, matte periwinkle, a calm periwinkle ink by night. Ingenium and Carpe Diem have newer builds than the copies Build 8 was measured from (both of 1 November 2026): for any further parity work, ask the owner for the newest builds first.
- **Writing:** No dashes in prose; short sentences; truth first; mark uncertainty.
- **Security:** Never read Android signing keys. The Fire key must be a GitHub secret (`EMPORIUM_ANDROID_KEY`, `EMPORIUM_ANDROID_KEY_PASSWORD`). On 14 November 2026, at the owner's request, a lasting key was made (4096-bit RSA, PKCS #12, alias `lakay`) and sent to the owner only, with the steps to add the two secrets; no copy was kept and it is in no repository. Until the owner adds them, each Fire build is signed with a one-build key. This session could not write repository secrets itself.
- **Departments and signs:** each department's sign is a line drawing of what it holds (a disc, a film, a bottle, a basket, a cup, a covered dish, two arrows, a tag) in the logo's jewel; never fruit or food as decoration (the coconut and dumpling emoji were removed in Build 15).
- **Transfers and top-ups:** never store a customer's name, address or papers; the provider keeps those. Keep the money apart from the sales. A mistake is voided with a reason (those who see the reports), never erased.
- **GitHub actions:** only releases written for Node.js 24 (or later), each pinned by its full commit with the version beside it, checked in its own `action.yml` first; never a bare label such as `@v4` (the owner, 14 November 2026). The same goes for every app, the search app included. On 14 November 2026 the owner's other apps' repositories were changed the same way, on the branch each builds from (list in `release/ACTIONS.md`). `DevGroup2313/Inkwell-Windows` was not reachable from this session and is still to check.
- **Process:** No pull requests unless asked. Give the owner a summary, download links and a handoff prompt with every release.
