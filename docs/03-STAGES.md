# Emporium: the stages

Emporium is built in stages. Each stage ends with a numbered build that you can install and check yourself, with the
packages for every device and the standalone page. Nothing moves to the next stage until the checks of the current one
pass.

Builds are numbered 1, 2, 3 and so on. The number is on the door, in About, at the foot of every report, in the name of
every package and on the GitHub release.

## Stage 0: the plan (7 November 2026)

- The audit of your four apps and its recommendations (`01-AUDIT-AND-RECOMMENDATIONS.md`).
- The design and the security model (`02-DESIGN-AND-SECURITY.md`).
- This plan, and the open questions (`04-QUESTIONS.md`).

**You check:** that the plan matches your vision; your answers to the questions.

## Stage 1: the foundation (Build 1)

- Emporium's identity: its seal, its colours, its type, Day, Night and Auto.
- English, French and Kreyòl, switchable at the door and anywhere in the app.
- The door: setting up the store, the owner's password, the Master Key (shown once, confirmed, printable).
- The books sealed on the device; locking by hand and when idle; "Verify the books".
- Inventory: items with categories, prices, costs, barcodes and reorder levels; receiving stock; correcting stock with a
  reason; archiving.
- The register: search or scan, the ticket, sales tax, cash with change due, the receipt.
- Reports: today, yesterday, this week, last week, this month, last month, this year, or any dates; profit; best sellers;
  sales by category and by hour; stock value; low stock; CSV export.
- Sealed backup and restore.
- Tips beside the controls, and the first Guidebook articles.
- Packages: the standalone page and the Windows installer first (your counter), then Mac (Apple silicon and Intel),
  iPhone and iPad, and the Fire tablet.

**You check:** set up a practice store, add ten items, receive stock, sell, read the day's report, lock, unlock with the
password, unlock with the Master Key, save a backup and restore it on another device.

**Build 2** (the same stage): the cohesive redesign, the store's name at the top of every page, and the Author Studio
(names, words, look, receipt, safekeeping). **You check:** rename the app and the store, rewrite a phrase, choose a set
of colours, word the receipt; lock and see the door wear the new names; export the choices, reset, and import them back.

## Stage 2: people and accountability (Build 2 and after)

- Staff profiles: manager and employees, with PINs; switching the person at the register; permissions you set.
- The cash drawer: opening count, cash movements, blind closing count, differences, manager sign off.
- Voids, refunds and discounts, with reasons and approvals.
- The ledger viewer: every line, filtered by person, day or item; any earlier day as it stood.
- Held sales ("Where was I?"); practice mode; importing an inventory list from a spreadsheet (CSV).
- The morning door and the day's closing with its seal.

## Stage 3: time and schedules

- Clock in and out, breaks timed with Clepsydra and Custodia.
- Time cards: corrections with a reason and an approval; weekly and pay period totals; export for payroll.
- Schedules: a weekly plan, templates, shift swaps and requests, approvals, changes recorded.
- Labour cost against sales.

## Stage 4: services, fresh food, suppliers

- Money transfers (RIA, CAM, Unitransfer) and Boss Revolution: amount, fee, commission and reference; the money held
  for each provider kept apart from the store's income; settlement and reconciliation.
- Patties and fresh food: made or bought, sold, thrown away.
- Suppliers, purchase orders, deliveries checked against orders.
- Stock counts and shrinkage; perfume and other high-value items with serial numbers and shift counts.
- Reminders: reorders, settlements, deliveries.

## Stage 5: every device in step

- An encrypted relay on the internet (your choice of service), holding only sealed data.
- Adding a device with the owner's approval; removing a lost device.
- The owner's view of the store from anywhere.

## Stage 6: the tools and the full tour

- Tabula, Abacus and Imago; sticky notes beside Clepsydra; floating windows; the Quiet Screen.
- The guided tour with live demonstrations in the practice store; read aloud; the complete Guidebook.

## Stage 7: payments and hardware

- Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay through the card system you choose (Square or another).
- Receipt printer, barcode labels, cash drawer, a screen facing customers.

## Stage 8: travel bookings

- Clients, reservations, itineraries, deposits, balances due, commissions, documents, reminders.

## Stage 9: ready for audit

- Builds signed with your key; key rotation; a demonstration edition with invented data.
- A new store kit: Emporium set up for another store from a template (the Author Studio's choices, categories, tax
  rates, staff roles), with that store's own books, password and Master Key.
- Documentation for auditors; an independent security review; code signing for Mac, Windows and iPhone if you choose it.
